Math 252, 8.3 Trigonometric Integrals

  1. 1.

    We are interested in this chapter in integrals of the form

    ∫sinm⁡x⁢cosn⁡x⁢d⁢xand∫secm⁡x⁢tann⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Depending on the values of m and n, the strategy for breaking down the integrand changes. In each case:

    • •

      We will give the main tools, usually trigonometric identities, used for that type of integral.

    • •

      We will give an integration strategy. Note: In some cases, more than one strategy applies. The answers produced may look different, but they may be equivalent.

    • •

      You are responsible for figuring out a strategy to handle cotm⁡x⁢cscn⁡x.

  2. 2.

    sinm⁡x⁢cosn⁡x, where either m or n is odd.

    Odd Power Strategy.
    • •

      Main Tool: Pythagorean Identity,

      sin2⁡x+cos2⁡x=1.
    • •

      Step 1: Whichever exponent is odd, take out one power. For example,

      cos5⁡x=cos4⁡x⁢cos⁡x

      or

      cos5⁡x⁢sin3⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢sin2⁡x⁢sin⁡x.
    • •

      Step 2: Apply the Pythagorean Identity to the exponent that has been made even.

      cos5⁡x=cos4⁡x⁢cos⁡x=(cos2⁡x⏟1−sin2⁡x)2⁢cos⁡x=(1−sin2⁡x)2⁢cos⁡x.
      cos5⁡x⁢sin3⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢sin2⁡x⏟1−cos2⁡x⁢sin⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢(1−cos2⁡x)⁢sin⁡x.
    • •

      Step 3: Simplify and use u-substitution.

  3. 3.

    Example 1: Evaluate ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution:

    ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sin2⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1−cos2⁡x)⁢cos4⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(cos4⁡x−cos6⁡x)⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Let u=cos⁡x. Then d⁢u=−sin⁡x⁢d⁢x, so −d⁢u=sin⁡x⁢d⁢x. Therefore,

    −∫(u4−u6)⁢𝑑u=−u55+u77+C.

    Hence,

    ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x=cos7⁡x7−cos5⁡x5+C.
  4. 4.

    Example 2: Evaluate ∫sin5⁡x⁢cos3⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution:

    ∫sin5⁡x⁢cos3⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sin5⁡x⁢cos2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫sin5⁡x⁢(1−sin2⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(sin5⁡x−sin7⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =sin6⁡x6−sin8⁡x8+C.
  5. 5.

    Example 3: Evaluate ∫cos3⁡xsin⁡x⁢𝑑x.

    Solution:

    ∫cos3⁡xsin⁡x⁢𝑑x =∫cos3⁡x⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫cos2⁡x⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1−sin2⁡x)⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(sin−1/2⁡x−sin3/2⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =2⁢sin1/2⁡x−25⁢sin5/2⁡x+C.
  6. 6.

    sinm⁡x⁢cosn⁡x, where m and n are both even.

    Even Power Strategy.
    • •

      Main Tool: Half-angle formulas,

      sin2⁡x=1−cos⁡2⁢x2andcos2⁡x=1+cos⁡2⁢x2.
    • •

      Step 1: Write each factor as a power of sin2⁡x or cos2⁡x.

      cos4⁡x=(cos2⁡x)2

      or

      sin6⁡x⁢cos2⁡x=(sin2⁡x)3⁢cos2⁡x.
    • •

      Step 2: Apply the half-angle formulas and simplify. The goal is to reduce the integrand to an expression that can be integrated.

      cos4⁡x=(cos2⁡x)2=(1+cos⁡2⁢x2)2.
      sin6⁡x⁢cos2⁡x=(sin2⁡x)3⁢cos2⁡x=(1−cos⁡2⁢x2)3⁢(1+cos⁡2⁢x2).
    • •

      Step 3: Continue to apply half-angle formulas until the problem is reduced to something that can be integrated.

  7. 7.

    Example 4: Evaluate ∫cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution:

    ∫cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫(cos2⁡x)2⁢𝑑x
    =∫(1+cos⁡2⁢x2)2⁢𝑑x
    =∫(14+cos⁡2⁢x2+cos2⁡2⁢x4)⁢𝑑x
    =∫[14+cos⁡2⁢x2+1+cos⁡4⁢x8]⁢𝑑x
    =∫(3+4⁢cos⁡2⁢x+cos⁡4⁢x8)⁢𝑑x
    =3⁢x8+sin⁡2⁢x4+sin⁡4⁢x32+C.
  8. 8.

    tanm⁡x⁢secn⁡x, for any m or n.

    Tangent-Secant Strategy.
    • •

      Main Tools: Pythagorean Identity,

      tan2⁡x+1=sec2⁡x,

      or the quotient and reciprocal identities,

      tan⁡x=sin⁡xcos⁡xandsec⁡x=1cos⁡x.
    • •

      Option 1: Convert to a product of a polynomial in tan⁡x and sec2⁡x, then use u-substitution.

    • •

      Option 2: Convert to a polynomial in sec⁡x times sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x, then use u-substitution.

    • •

      Option 3: Convert to expressions involving sin⁡x and cos⁡x, then use the methods listed above.

  9. 9.

    Example 5: Evaluate ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution: We will use Option 1.

    ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sec2⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1+tan2⁡x)⁢tan3⁡x⁢sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(tan3⁡x+tan5⁡x)⁢sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =tan4⁡x4+tan6⁡x6+C.
  10. 10.

    Example 6: Evaluate ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution: We will use Option 2.

    ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sec3⁡x⁢tan2⁡x⁢sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫sec3⁡x⁢(sec2⁡x−1)⁢sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(sec5⁡x−sec3⁡x)⁢sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =sec6⁡x6−sec4⁡x4+C.
  11. 11.

    Example 7: Evaluate ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    Solution: We will use Option 3.

    ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫(1cos⁡x)4⁢(sin⁡xcos⁡x)3⁢𝑑x
    =∫sin3⁡x⁢cos−7⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫sin2⁡x⁢cos−7⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1−cos2⁡x)⁢cos−7⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(cos−7⁡x−cos−5⁡x)⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =cos−6⁡x6−cos−4⁡x4+C
    =sec6⁡x6−sec4⁡x4+C.
