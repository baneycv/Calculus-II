    • •

      Main Tool Pythagorean Identity, sin2⁡x+cos2⁡x=1.

    • •

      Step 1: Which ever exponent odd, take out one power. For example,

      cos5⁡x=cos4⁡x⁢cos⁡x

      OR

      cos5⁡x⁢sin3⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢sin2⁡x⁢sin⁡x

    • •

      Step 2: Apply the Pythagorean Identity to the exponent that has been made even

      cos5⁡x=cos4⁡x⁢cos⁡x=(cos2⁡x⏟1−sin2⁡x)2⁢cos⁡x=(1−sin2⁡x)2⁢cos⁡x

      cos5⁡x⁢sin3⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢sin2⁡x⏟1−cos2⁡x⁢sin⁡x=cos5⁡x⁢(1−cos2⁡x)⁢sin⁡x

    • •

      Step 3: Simplify and use u-substitution

  3. 3.

    Example A: Evaluate ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x.
    Solution:

    ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sin2⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1−cos2⁡x)⁢cos4⁡x⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(cos4⁡x−cos6⁡x)⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x

    Let u=cos⁡x, d⁢u=sin⁡x⁢d⁢x, which means that −d⁢u=sin⁡x⁢d⁢x.

    −∫(u4−u6)⁢𝑑u=−u55+u77+C

    Hence,

    ∫sin3⁡x⁢cos4⁡x⁢d⁢x=cos7⁡x7−cos5⁡x5+C

    Example B: Evaluate ∫sin5⁡x⁢cos3⁡x⁢d⁢x.
    Solution:

    ∫sin5⁡x⁢cos3⁡x⁢d⁢x =∫sin5⁡x⁢cos2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫sin5⁡x⁢(1−sin2⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(sin5⁡x−sin7⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =sin6⁡x6−sin8⁡x8+C

    Example C: Evaluate ∫cos3⁡xsin⁡x⁢𝑑x.
    Solution:

    ∫cos3⁡xsin⁡x⁢𝑑x =∫cos3⁡x⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫cos2⁡x⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(1−sin2⁡x)⁢sin−1/2⁡x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =∫(sin−1/2⁡x−sin3/2⁡x)⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    =2⁢sin1/2⁡x−25⁢sin5/2⁡x+C

    • •

      Step 1: Write both terms as some power of cos2⁡x or sin2,

      cos4⁡x=(cos2⁡x)2

      OR

      sin6⁡x⁢cos2⁡x=(sin2⁡x)3⁢cos2⁡x

    • •

      Step 2: Apply the Half Angle Formula and simplify. Goal is to reduce to an expression that can be integrated

      cos4⁡x=(cos2⁡x)2=(1+cos⁡2⁢x2)2

      sin6⁡x⁢cos2⁡x=(sin2⁡x)3⁢cos2⁡x=(1−cos⁡2⁢x2)3⁢(1+cos⁡2⁢x2)

    • •

      Step 3: Continue to apply the Half-Angle Formulas until the problem is reduced to something that can be integrated

    Example E-1 Evaluate ∫sec4⁡x⁢tan3⁡x⁢d⁢x.
