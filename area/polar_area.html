Math 252, 10.5, Area Polar Regions

  1. 1.

    Area of a Sector A sector of a circle is a region of the circle subtended by an angle θ

    Since the sector represents the fraction θ2⁢π of the entire circle,

    Asector =θ2⁢π⁢(π⁢r2)
    =12⁢r2⁢θ.
  2. 2.

    Polar Regions. Consider the function r=f⁢(θ), for α<θ<β.

    Partition the interval [α,β] into n subintervals of width

    Δ⁢θ=β−αn.

    On the ith subinterval, choose a sample angle θi∗. The corresponding thin polar region is approximately a sector with radius f⁢(θi∗) and central angle Δ⁢θ, so

    Δ⁢Ai≈12⁢[f⁢(θi∗)]2⁢Δ⁢θ.

    Adding the areas of the sectors gives

    A≈∑i=1n12⁢[f⁢(θi∗)]2⁢Δ⁢θ.

    Taking the limit as n→∞ gives the Riemann sum

    A =limn→∞∑i=1n12⁢[f⁢(θi∗)]2⁢Δ⁢θ
    =∫αβ12⁢[f⁢(θ)]2⁢𝑑θ.
  3. 3.

    Area of a Polar Region.. If f is continuous and nonnegative on the interval [α,β], 0<β−α<2⁢π, then the area of the region of the area bounded between α and β is given by,

    ∫αβ12⁢[f⁢(θ)]2⁢𝑑θ

  5. 5.

    Example 1: Determine the area one petal of the graph of r=3⁢cos⁡3⁢θ.

    The petal centered on the polar axis begins and ends where r=0:

    3⁢cos⁡3⁢θ=0⟹θ=−π6,π6.

    Therefore,

    A =12⁢∫−π/6π/6(3⁢cos⁡3⁢θ)2⁢𝑑θ
    =92⁢∫−π/6π/6cos2⁡3⁢θ⁢d⁢θ
    =92⁢∫−π/6π/61+cos⁡6⁢θ2⁢𝑑θ
    =94⁢[θ+16⁢sin⁡6⁢θ]−π/6π/6
    =3⁢π4.
  6. 6.

    Example 2: Determine the area of the outter loop of the limaçon with inner loop given by r=1−2⁢sin⁡θ.

    First determine where the curve passes through the pole:

    1−2⁢sin⁡θ =0
    sin⁡θ =12,

    so θ=π6 and θ=5⁢π6. The outer loop is traced from θ=5⁢π6 to θ=13⁢π6. Thus,

    A =12⁢∫5⁢π/613⁢π/6(1−2⁢sin⁡θ)2⁢𝑑θ
    =12⁢∫5⁢π/613⁢π/6(1−4⁢sin⁡θ+4⁢sin2⁡θ)⁢𝑑θ
    =12⁢∫5⁢π/613⁢π/6(3−4⁢sin⁡θ−2⁢cos⁡2⁢θ)⁢𝑑θ
    =12⁢[3⁢θ+4⁢cos⁡θ−sin⁡2⁢θ]5⁢π/613⁢π/6
    =2⁢π+3⁢32.
  7. 7.

    Example 3: Determine the area of the region bounded between the cardioid r=2−2⁢cos⁡θ and the circle r=−6⁢cos⁡θ.

    Find the angles at which the curves intersect:

    2−2⁢cos⁡θ =−6⁢cos⁡θ
    2 =−4⁢cos⁡θ
    cos⁡θ =−12.

    Thus, the curves intersect at θ=2⁢π3 and θ=4⁢π3. By symmetry about the polar axis, we may find the area above the axis and double it. On [π2,2⁢π3], the circle is the inner curve, and on [2⁢π3,π], the cardioid is the inner curve. Therefore,

    A =2⁢[12⁢∫π/22⁢π/3(−6⁢cos⁡θ)2⁢𝑑θ+12⁢∫2⁢π/3π(2−2⁢cos⁡θ)2⁢𝑑θ]
    =∫π/22⁢π/336⁢cos2⁡θ⁢d⁢θ+∫2⁢π/3π(2−2⁢cos⁡θ)2⁢𝑑θ
    =2⁢[(3⁢π2−9⁢34)+(π+9⁢34)]
    =5⁢π.
