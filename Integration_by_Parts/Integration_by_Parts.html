Math 252, 8.2 Integration by Parts

  1. 1.

    Derivation. Consider two differentiable functions, u⁢(x) and v⁢(x). Later, I will drop the x for less cumbersome notation. Recall the product rule,

    (u⁢v)′=u⁢d⁢v+v⁢d⁢u

    Now, suppose we want to undo the differentiation. The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus says that the integral undoes differentiation and differentiation undoes integration. So to “undo” differentiation, we need to integrate both sides of the equation.

    ∫(u⁢v)′⁢𝑑x = ∫(u′⁢v+v′⁢u)⁢𝑑x
    u⁢v = ∫u⁢𝑑v+∫v⁢𝑑u

    If we exchange the u⁢v, and the first term on the right, we end up with the integration by parts formula,

    ∫u⁢𝑑v=u⁢v−∫v⁢𝑑u
    • •

      This formula is among the MOST important integration techniques in all of math and science.

    • •

      It is primarily used to integrate problems where you have an integrand that is the product of an algebraic function and transcendental function (e.g. xr⁢ex, xr⁢ln⁡x, xr⁢tan⁡x, xr⁢sin⁡x, etc.)

    • •

      In order to use it, we have to assign one function to be u and the other to be d⁢v. Once we have done this, then we find d⁢u and v and plug it into the formula.

    • •

      The integration by parts formula is sometimes called a reduction formula because it reduces the order or degree.

    • •

      Good choices for u are xr when r≥1, or ln⁡x. In general, we want the r to be get smaller as we apply the technique.

    • •

      Good choices of d⁢v are trigonometric functions (for which we can INTEGRATE). Remember You are going to have to find the anti-derivative of d⁢v, and so you will generally want to choose d⁢v so that it is EASY to integrate. xr for r≤−1 is also a good choice of d⁢v.

  2. 2.

    Example 1: Evaluate ∫x⁢ex⁢𝑑x.
    Solution: We let u=x, so that leaves d⁢v=ex⁢d⁢x. This means then that d⁢u=d⁢x, and v=ex. Remember to find d⁢u, we differentiate u and to find v we integrate d⁢v. Using the formula, now we have that,

    ∫x⁢ex⁢𝑑x = x⁢ex−∫ex⁢𝑑x
    = x⁢ex−ex+C
  3. 3.

    Example 2: Evaluate ∫x2⁢ln⁡x⁢d⁢x.
    Solution: Remember, our overall goal is to reduce the order of the problem. In addition, we want choices of u and d⁢v that simplify our lives. Let u=ln⁡x and d⁢v=x2⁢d⁢x (Try it the other way and see what happens). This means then that d⁢u=1x⁢d⁢x and v=x33. Thus, you have

    ∫x2⁢ln⁡x⁢d⁢x = x3⁢ln⁡x3−∫(x33)⁢(1x)⁢𝑑x
    = x3⁢ln⁡x3−∫x23⁢𝑑x
    = x3⁢ln⁡x3−x39+C
  4. 4.

    Example 3: Evaluate ∫arcsin⁡x⁢d⁢x.
    Solution: In this case our integrand is the product of arcsin⁡x and 1⁢d⁢x. Clearly, the latter can’t be u because, if we differentiated we would get 0. We can’t let d⁢v equal the former because that’s the problem (finding the integral). Hence, let u=arcsin⁡x and d⁢v=1⁢d⁢x. Thus, d⁢u=11−x2 and v=x. This leads to,

    ∫arcsin⁡x⁢d⁢x = x⁢arcsin⁡x−∫x1−x2⁢𝑑x
    = x⁢arcsin⁡x−(−12)⁢∫−2⁢x⁢d⁢x1−x2
    = x⁢arcsin⁡x−1−x2+C

  5. 5.

    Example 4: Evaluate ∫x2⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    Solution: Let u=x2 and d⁢v=sin⁡x⁢d⁢x. Hence, we get d⁢u=2⁢x⁢d⁢x and v=−cos⁡x.

    ∫x2⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x=−x2⁢cos⁡x+2⁢∫x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x

    Now we have to do integration by parts again, so we end up with, u=x, d⁢v=cos⁡x and d⁢u=d⁢x and v=sin⁡x. Hence we now have,

    ∫x2⁢sin⁡x⁢d⁢x = −x2⁢cos⁡x+∫x⁢cos⁡x⁢d⁢x
    = −x2⁢cos⁡x+x⁢sin⁡x−∫sin⁡x⁢d⁢x
    = −x2⁢cos⁡x+2⁢x⁢sin⁡x+2⁢cos⁡x+C
  6. 6.

    Example 5: Evaluate ∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x.
    Solution: In tis problem, we have to be a little bit more clever. We can split the integrand up, into sec⁡x⁢sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x. Note that we can now integrate the second factor. Hence, u=sec⁡x, d⁢v=sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x and d⁢u=sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x⁢d⁢x while v=tan⁡x. Thus,

    ∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x = ∫sec⁡x⁢sec2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−∫tan⁡x⁢(sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x⁢d⁢x)
    = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−∫sec⁡x⁢tan2⁡x⁢d⁢x
    = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−∫sec⁡x⁢(sec2⁡x−1)⁢𝑑x
    = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x−∫sec⁡x⁢d⁢x

    We move the middle term to the other side and we get,

    2⁢∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−∫sec⁡x⁢d⁢x
    2⁢∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x = sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−ln⁡|sec⁡x+tan⁡x|+C
    ∫sec3⁡x⁢d⁢x = 12⁢(sec⁡x⁢tan⁡x−ln⁡|sec⁡x+tan⁡x|)+C
  7. 7.

    Example 6: Let D be the solid formed by revolving the graph of f⁢(x)=e−2⁢x for 0≤x≤2 around the y-axis. Determine the volume of D.
    Solution:

    V=2⁢π⁢∫02x⁢e−2⁢x⁢𝑑x

    Use integration by parts:

    u=x,d⁢v=e−2⁢x⁢d⁢x
    d⁢u=d⁢x,v=−12⁢e−2⁢x

    So

    ∫x⁢e−2⁢x⁢𝑑x=−x2⁢e−2⁢x+12⁢∫e−2⁢x⁢𝑑x
    =−x2⁢e−2⁢x−14⁢e−2⁢x

    Therefore,

    V=2⁢π⁢[−x2⁢e−2⁢x−14⁢e−2⁢x]02
    V=2⁢π⁢[(−1−14)⁢e−4−(−14)]
    V=2⁢π⁢[14−54⁢e−4]
