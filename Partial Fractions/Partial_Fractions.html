Math 252, 8.5 Partial Fractions

  1. 1.

    Motivation We now turn to partial fraction decompositions which will enable us to integrate the following

    ∫d⁢x(x2−4)2,∫d⁢x(x−3)⁢(x+4),∫x3x3−8
  2. 2.

    Preparation 1: When applicable, divide the numerator of the rational function by its denominator, to ensure that the degree of the numerator of any resulting remainder is less than the degree of the denominator.

    1. (a)

      Example 1: Perform (i) on 2⁢x3x2+3.

      Since the degree of the numerator is greater than the degree of the denominator, we begin with polynomial division. Dividing the leading terms gives

      2⁢x3x2=2⁢x.

      We multiply the divisor by 2⁢x and subtract:

      2⁢x3−(2⁢x)⁢(x2+3) =2⁢x3−(2⁢x3+6⁢x)
      =−6⁢x.

      Therefore the quotient is 2⁢x and the remainder is −6⁢x, so

      2⁢x3x2+3 =2⁢x+−6⁢xx2+3
      =2⁢x−6⁢xx2+3.

      Notice that the remainder −6⁢x has degree 1, which is less than the degree 2 of the denominator. Thus no further polynomial division is necessary.

    2. (b)

      Example 2: Perform (i) on x2−1x2+1.

      Again, the numerator and denominator have the same degree, so polynomial division is necessary. Dividing the leading terms gives

      x2x2=1.

      We multiply the divisor by 1 and subtract:

      (x2−1)−(x2+1) =x2−1−x2−1
      =−2.

      Therefore the quotient is 1 and the remainder is −2, so

      x2−1x2+1 =1+−2x2+1
      =1−2x2+1.

      The remainder is a constant, so its degree is less than the degree of the denominator. The rational expression is now in the form required for the next step.

    Preparation 2: Factor the numerator and the denominator of the remainder into linear and quadratic factors. That is into expressions of the form,

    constants,(a⁢x+b)r,(a⁢x2+b⁢x+c)s

    where r and s are positive integers. Finally reduce the fraction further by crossing out common factors from the numerator and denominator.

    Example 4: Evaluate ∫1x2−8⁢x+15⁢𝑑x.

    We begin by factoring the denominator:

    x2−8⁢x+15=(x−3)⁢(x−5).

    Thus

    1(x−3)⁢(x−5)=Ax−3+Bx−5.

    Multiplying both sides by (x−3)⁢(x−5) gives

    1=A⁢(x−5)+B⁢(x−3).

    We now choose convenient values of x:

    x=3: 1=−2⁢A⟹A=−12,
    x=5: 1=2⁢B⟹B=12.

    Therefore

    1x2−8⁢x+15=−12⁢(x−3)+12⁢(x−5).

    Hence

    ∫1x2−8⁢x+15⁢𝑑x =−12⁢∫1x−3⁢𝑑x+12⁢∫1x−5⁢𝑑x
    =−12⁢ln⁡|x−3|+12⁢ln⁡|x−5|+C.

    Thus

    12⁢ln⁡|x−5x−3|+C.

    Example 5: Evaluate ∫x2+2⁢x+7x3+x2−2⁢𝑑x

    First factor the denominator. Since x=1 is a zero,

    x3+x2−2 =(x−1)⁢(x2+2⁢x+2).

    The quadratic factor is irreducible over the real numbers, so the partial fraction decomposition has the form

    x2+2⁢x+7(x−1)⁢(x2+2⁢x+2)=Ax−1+B⁢x+Cx2+2⁢x+2.

    Multiplying by (x−1)⁢(x2+2⁢x+2) gives

    x2+2⁢x+7=A⁢(x2+2⁢x+2)+(B⁢x+C)⁢(x−1).

    Expanding the right-hand side,

    x2+2⁢x+7 =A⁢x2+2⁢A⁢x+2⁢A+B⁢x2−B⁢x+C⁢x−C
    =(A+B)⁢x2+(2⁢A−B+C)⁢x+(2⁢A−C).

    Equating coefficients gives

    A+B =1,
    2⁢A−B+C =2,
    2⁢A−C =7.

    Solving this system yields

    A=2,B=−1,C=−3.

    Therefore

    x2+2⁢x+7x3+x2−2=2x−1−x+3x2+2⁢x+2.

    Thus

    ∫x2+2⁢x+7x3+x2−2⁢𝑑x =2⁢∫1x−1⁢𝑑x−∫x+3x2+2⁢x+2⁢𝑑x.

    For the second integral, write

    x+3=(x+1)+2,

    and note that

    x2+2⁢x+2=(x+1)2+1.

    Hence

    ∫x2+2⁢x+7x3+x2−2⁢𝑑x =2⁢ln⁡|x−1|−∫x+1x2+2⁢x+2⁢𝑑x−2⁢∫1(x+1)2+1⁢𝑑x
    =2⁢ln⁡|x−1|−12⁢ln⁡(x2+2⁢x+2)−2⁢arctan⁡(x+1)+C.

    Therefore

    2⁢ln⁡|x−1|−12⁢ln⁡(x2+2⁢x+2)−2⁢arctan⁡(x+1)+C.
  8. 8.

    Example 6: Evaluate ∫1x⁢(x2+1)2⁢𝑑x

    The denominator contains a linear factor and a repeated irreducible quadratic factor. Therefore we write

    1x⁢(x2+1)2=Ax+B⁢x+Cx2+1+D⁢x+E(x2+1)2.

    Multiplying by x⁢(x2+1)2 gives

    1=A⁢(x2+1)2+(B⁢x+C)⁢x⁢(x2+1)+(D⁢x+E)⁢x.

    Expanding,

    1 =A⁢(x4+2⁢x2+1)+(B⁢x+C)⁢(x3+x)+D⁢x2+E⁢x
    =(A+B)⁢x4+C⁢x3+(2⁢A+B+D)⁢x2+(C+E)⁢x+A.

    Equating coefficients gives

    A+B =0,
    C =0,
    2⁢A+B+D =0,
    C+E =0,
    A =1.

    Therefore

    A=1,B=−1,C=0,D=−1,E=0.

    Thus

    1x⁢(x2+1)2=1x−xx2+1−x(x2+1)2.

    Now integrate term-by-term:

    ∫1x⁢(x2+1)2⁢𝑑x =∫1x⁢𝑑x−∫xx2+1⁢𝑑x−∫x(x2+1)2⁢𝑑x
    =ln⁡|x|−12⁢ln⁡(x2+1)+12⁢(x2+1)+C.

    Therefore

    ln⁡|x|−12⁢ln⁡(x2+1)+12⁢(x2+1)+C.
