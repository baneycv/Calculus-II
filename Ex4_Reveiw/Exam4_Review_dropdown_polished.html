  1. (1)

    Let the curve C be given by x=−3⁢sin⁡2⁢t and y=3⁢cos⁡2⁢t for 0≤t≤3⁢π.

    1. (a)

      By eliminating the parameter t, find the equation of the circle (centered at the origin) in rectangular coordinates.

      Solution

      Solution. Squaring the two parametric equations gives

      x2=9⁢sin2⁡(2⁢t)andy2=9⁢cos2⁡(2⁢t).

      Therefore,

      x2+y2=9⁢(sin2⁡(2⁢t)+cos2⁡(2⁢t))=9.

      Thus, the rectangular equation is

      x2+y2=9.
    2. (b)

      Determine the direction in which the circle is traversed and the number of revolutions completed over the interval.

      Solution

      Solution. The equations may be rewritten as

      x=3⁢cos⁡(π2+2⁢t),y=3⁢sin⁡(π2+2⁢t).

      Hence the polar angle is

      θ=π2+2⁢t,

      which increases as t increases. Therefore, the circle is traversed counterclockwise.

      As t increases from 0 to 3⁢π, the angle increases by

      Δ⁢θ=2⁢(3⁢π)−2⁢(0)=6⁢π.

      Since one revolution corresponds to an angle of 2⁢π,

      6⁢π2⁢π=3.

      Thus, the curve is traversed counterclockwise for

      3⁢ complete revolutions.

    Let the curve C be given by x=−2+3⁢t and y=2−3⁢t for all t.

    1. (a)

      By eliminating the parameter t, determine an equation relating x and y.

      Solution

      Solution. From x=−2+3⁢t, we obtain

      3⁢t=x+2.

      Substituting this into the equation for y gives

      y=2−3⁢t=2−(x+2)=−x.

      Therefore, an equation relating x and y is

      y=−x,

      or equivalently, x+y=0.

    2. (b)

      Sketch a graph of C, indicating the direction.

      Solution

      Solution. Because

      d⁢xd⁢t=3>0andd⁢yd⁢t=−3<0,

      the x-coordinate increases and the y-coordinate decreases as t increases. Thus, the line is traversed from the upper left toward the lower right.

      Solution

      Solution. The component functions have continuous derivatives on [−2,2]:

      d⁢xd⁢t=3⁢t2andd⁢yd⁢t=4⁢t3.

      d⁢xd⁢t=0andd⁢yd⁢t=0.
